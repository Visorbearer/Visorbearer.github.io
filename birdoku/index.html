Thanks for checking it out!

How to Play

Fill each square with a bird species that matches both the row category and the column category.

Each bird can only be used once. As you type, the game will suggest matching species.

Once you feel confident in your selections, submit your answers by clicking the "Submit" button.

After submitting, your results will be displayed. Green squares indicate correct answers and red squares incorrect answers. Hover over submitted answers to see how that bird fits into the two categories.

Your submission is saved locally in your browser, so you can only play each day once.
