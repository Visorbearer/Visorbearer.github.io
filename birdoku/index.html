How to Play

Fill each square with a bird species that matches both the row category and the column category.

You can click any row or column category to see other possible options in that category group. Many birds fall under multiple options within a category.

Each bird can only be used once. As you type, the game will suggest matching species.

Once you feel confident in all your selections, submit your answers by clicking the "Submit" button.

After submitting, your results will be displayed. Green indicates correct answers and red indicates incorrect answers. Hover over or tap on answers to see how that bird fits into the two categories.

Your submission is saved locally in your browser, so you can only play each day once.

Submit an incomplete Birdoku?

Some tiles are still empty. You can go back and keep filling them in or submit now.

Welcome to Endless Mode!

Here, you can continuously play randomly generated Birdoku puzzles to your heart's content.

Note that completing these puzzles will not affect your streak score.

After submitting your answers in Endless Mode, you can press the "Play Again" button to replay the same puzzle or "Play Another" to play a different one.

If you'd like to go back to daily mode, click the "Daily" button in the header.
